I'm a fourth-year PhD student at Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), Georgia Tech advised by [Prof. Tushar Krishna](https://tusharkrishna.ece.gatech.edu). My research broadly focuses on hardware–software co-design for efficient AI systems, with a primary focus on AI accelerator simulation, architecture design, and design-space exploration. More recently, my work has expanded to AI compiler optimization and system-level optimization for agentic AI, including heterogeneous scheduling and model routing.


I received my Bachelor’s in Technology (BTech) with distrinction in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Roorkee, India in 2023, where I served as the student representative in the Department Academic Program Committe (DAPC) representing over 500 undergraduate students. As a DAPC representative, I founded a student society to organize ECE talks and events and foster student-faculty collaboration.
